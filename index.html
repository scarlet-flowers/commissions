    About Me

    Hello there! I’m scarlet-flowers, and this is my commissions gallery. Big thank you to Yoostink for helping me get this set up as I am terribly inept with technology.

    As you can probably tell, I’m a big Awakening fan! Every run, I play as female Robin and marry Chrom because I love their dynamic and what it does for the story. I also tend to pair Lucina with Inigo (fathered by Virion) and male Morgan with Severa (fathered by Gaius). Thanks to my partner’s influence, I’m also fond of male Robin and Sumia, and I like to pair Cynthia with Owain (fathered by Vaike) and female Morgan with Gerome (fathered by Frederick). So those are the main pairings you’ll see on here!

    Outside of that, I’m also a big Kagamine Len fan. If I’m not doing an Awakening commission, it’ll most likely be of him. You can also see [here](https://imgur.com/a/LrakgZR) for any series/characters that I’m fond of.

    Thank you for taking a looking at my gallery! I hope you enjoyed, and that it may inspire you to create or commission something of your own.
