    About Me

    Hello there! I’m scarlet-flowers, and this is my commissions gallery. Big thank you to Yoostink for helping me get this set up as I am terribly inept with technology.

    As you can probably tell, I’m a big Awakening fan! Every run, I play as female Robin and marry Chrom because I love their dynamic and what it does for the story. Thanks to my boyfriend’s influence, I am also fond of male Robin and Sumia. Awakening, and by extension Heroes, is how we initially bonded so this game really holds a special place in my heart.

    Other pairings you will see on here include Lucina and Inigo (with Soleil as their daughter), male Morgan and Severa (with Caeldori as their daughter), Cynthia and Owain (with Ophelia as their daughter), female Morgan and Gerome (with my OC Damien as their son), Virion and Olivia, Gaius and Cordelia, Vaike and Lissa, and Frederick and Cherche. You’ll also see a lot of commissions here that were done in collaboration with my partner-in-crime, LampreyLarry. Any original outfits you see are also my own design.

    Outside of that, I’m also a big Kagamine Len fan. If I’m not doing an Awakening commission, it’ll most likely be of him. You can also see here for any series/characters that I’m fond of.

    Thank you for taking a looking at my gallery! I hope you enjoyed, and that it may inspire you to create or commission something of your own.

    What if Naga was the antagonist?

    That is the main idea behind my roleswap AU. Instead of a Grima-possessed Robin, it’s a Naga-possessed Chrom. Instead of Risen King Chrom, it’s Ascended Queen Robin. It's Lucina who follows her parents and Morgan who goes back in time. Et cetera, et cetera.

    I’ve also been asked many times if I plan on ever writing a fanfic about this concept and my answer is basically “probably not?” While I have some loose ideas about the AU, it’s nothing I’m passionate about enough to put to paper as I’d actually prefer to funnel that creative energy towards other fandoms. So this is more of a vibes and aesthetics type AU, but one that I am very fond of and plan on continuing to get commissions for.

    I think this goes without saying, but please do not steal my designs without my permission. If you like this AU and want to expand on it more, feel free to hit me up and chat with me about it!
